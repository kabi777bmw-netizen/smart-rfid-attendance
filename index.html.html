Setup notes

  • This is a laptop browser app for a USB RFID reader that behaves like a keyboard. Keep the scan box focused.
  • The sample card IDs below must match the numbers your reader outputs. Replace them with your actual card IDs if different.
  • This browser page does not automatically send data to the Arduino/TFT. That needs a separate serial bridge or a backend connection.
  • Student photos and real-time TFT updates are not included in this starter version.
